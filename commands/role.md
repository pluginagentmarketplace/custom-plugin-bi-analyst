# /role - Explore BI Analyst Roles in Detail

Deep dive into Business Intelligence roles with detailed information about responsibilities, required skills, tools, and career opportunities.

## Core BI Roles

### BI Analyst
**Overview:** Transforms data into actionable business insights through analysis and visualization.

**Key Responsibilities:**
- Create and maintain dashboards and reports
- Analyze business data to identify trends
- Collaborate with stakeholders to understand requirements
- Develop KPIs and metrics frameworks
- Present findings to business users

**Required Skills:**
- SQL (Advanced)
- Excel (Expert)
- Power BI or Tableau (Proficient)
- Data modeling basics
- Business acumen
- Communication skills

**Tools & Technologies:**
- BI Platforms: Power BI, Tableau, Looker
- Databases: SQL Server, PostgreSQL, Snowflake
- Spreadsheets: Excel, Google Sheets
- Collaboration: Jira, Confluence

**Salary Range:** $65K-$120K | **Demand:** Very High

---

### Senior BI Analyst
**Overview:** Leads complex analytics projects and mentors junior analysts.

**Key Responsibilities:**
- Design enterprise-level reporting solutions
- Lead analytics projects end-to-end
- Mentor junior team members
- Define data standards and best practices
- Partner with leadership on strategic initiatives

**Additional Skills:**
- Data architecture knowledge
- Project management
- Stakeholder management
- Multiple BI tools proficiency
- Statistical analysis

**Salary Range:** $90K-$150K | **Demand:** High

---

### BI Developer
**Overview:** Builds and maintains BI infrastructure and solutions.

**Key Responsibilities:**
- Develop ETL/ELT pipelines
- Build semantic models and data marts
- Optimize report performance
- Implement data security
- Maintain BI platform infrastructure

**Required Skills:**
- SQL (Expert)
- ETL tools (SSIS, Azure Data Factory)
- Data modeling (Advanced)
- Python or scripting
- Cloud platforms

**Tools & Technologies:**
- ETL: SSIS, Informatica, Talend, dbt
- Cloud: Azure, AWS, GCP
- Databases: Data warehouses
- Version control: Git

**Salary Range:** $80K-$140K | **Demand:** High

---

### Data Visualization Specialist
**Overview:** Creates compelling visual stories from complex data.

**Key Responsibilities:**
- Design intuitive dashboards
- Develop visualization standards
- Create interactive reports
- Optimize user experience
- Train users on visualization tools

**Required Skills:**
- Tableau or Power BI (Expert)
- Design principles
- User experience (UX)
- Data storytelling
- Accessibility standards

**Salary Range:** $75K-$130K | **Demand:** High

---

### Analytics Engineer
**Overview:** Bridges the gap between data engineering and analytics.

**Key Responsibilities:**
- Build and maintain data models
- Create reusable analytics assets
- Implement data quality checks
- Document data lineage
- Optimize query performance

**Required Skills:**
- SQL (Expert)
- dbt or similar tools
- Python
- Data modeling
- Version control

**Tools & Technologies:**
- dbt, Dataform
- Snowflake, BigQuery, Redshift
- Git, CI/CD
- Airflow, Dagster

**Salary Range:** $100K-$160K | **Demand:** Very High

---

### BI Manager
**Overview:** Leads BI teams and drives analytics strategy.

**Key Responsibilities:**
- Manage BI team and resources
- Define analytics strategy
- Stakeholder relationship management
- Budget and vendor management
- Drive BI adoption across organization

**Required Skills:**
- Leadership and management
- Strategic thinking
- Business partnership
- Technical understanding
- Communication

**Salary Range:** $130K-$180K | **Demand:** Medium-High

---

### Data Architect (BI Focus)
**Overview:** Designs enterprise data architecture for analytics.

**Key Responsibilities:**
- Design data warehouse architecture
- Define data governance standards
- Plan technology roadmaps
- Ensure scalability and performance
- Guide team on technical decisions

**Required Skills:**
- Data architecture (Expert)
- Cloud platforms (Advanced)
- Data modeling (Expert)
- Data governance
- Enterprise thinking

**Salary Range:** $140K-$200K | **Demand:** High

---

## Specialized Roles

### Healthcare BI Analyst
**Focus:** Clinical and operational healthcare analytics
**Special Requirements:** HIPAA knowledge, healthcare domain expertise
**Tools:** Epic, Cerner integration, clinical dashboards

### Financial BI Analyst
**Focus:** Financial reporting and risk analytics
**Special Requirements:** Financial regulations, accounting knowledge
**Tools:** Bloomberg, financial modeling, regulatory reporting

### Marketing Analytics Analyst
**Focus:** Marketing performance and customer analytics
**Special Requirements:** Marketing domain, attribution modeling
**Tools:** Google Analytics, marketing platforms, customer data platforms

### Supply Chain BI Analyst
**Focus:** Logistics and operations analytics
**Special Requirements:** Supply chain knowledge, forecasting
**Tools:** SAP, Oracle, supply chain platforms

## Day in the Life

### BI Analyst Typical Day
- 9:00 - Review overnight data refreshes
- 9:30 - Standup meeting with team
- 10:00 - Work on dashboard development
- 12:00 - Lunch
- 13:00 - Stakeholder meeting for requirements
- 14:30 - SQL analysis for ad-hoc request
- 16:00 - Documentation and testing
- 17:00 - Plan tomorrow's priorities

### Senior BI Analyst Typical Day
- 9:00 - Review team's work and provide feedback
- 10:00 - Strategic planning meeting
- 11:00 - Architecture review session
- 12:00 - Lunch with stakeholder
- 13:30 - Mentor junior analyst
- 15:00 - Work on complex analysis
- 16:30 - Executive presentation prep
- 17:30 - Wrap up and planning

## How to Choose Your Role

### Choose BI Analyst if you:
- Enjoy working with business users
- Like variety in daily tasks
- Want balance of technical and business

### Choose BI Developer if you:
- Prefer technical challenges
- Enjoy building infrastructure
- Like solving performance problems

### Choose Analytics Engineer if you:
- Want modern data stack experience
- Enjoy writing clean, tested code
- Like building reusable solutions

### Choose Management if you:
- Enjoy leading and mentoring others
- Want to influence strategy
- Like business partnership
